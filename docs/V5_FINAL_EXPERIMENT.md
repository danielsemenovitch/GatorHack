# V5 final Western blot research experiment — registered before training

**Status:** Experimental; only compare after CI success. Not the live product. No automatic deployment. The primary hackathon feature remains figure duplication detection.

## Goal

Aim for **at least 50% sensitivity on preregistered readable, moderately sized AI-band edits at ≤5% clean false flags**, compared with frozen V3 **on the same held-out papers**. Also publish a difficult stress benchmark, including 50–70% downsampling and JPEG quality 60–78, regardless of performance. Never represent this restricted artificial benchmark as real-world forensic sensitivity.

## Architecture, data and labels

- Three per-patch inputs at 32 × 32: local relative intensity, high-frequency background-normalized residual, and multiscale broad contrast.
- Residual convolutional network with 24/32/48 feature channels, group normalization, average pooling, pooled average+max head; no pretrained or real-world forgery weights.
- Fixed six epochs, deterministic seed 0, AdamW cosine warm-up/decay, no automatic hyperparameter sweep. CPU-compatible.
- Restore up to **40 genuine band patches** per image and **8%** background sampling with cap 24 per image, vs V4.1's 20 / 3.5%.
- Edit-mask patch rule lowered from ≥25% edited pixels to **≥3.5%**, exclude ambiguous partly edited patches from *negative* sampling rather than mislabeling them as clean.
- Document training images that contain no eligible positive patches. Stop training if more than 23% of edited training images lack eligible positives.
- Same CC BY public source papers as V4 pipeline, rehydrated plus newly verified sources; 1,750 panels targeted from ≥180 independent papers. Test/train split by MD5(PMCID), calibration by CRC32(PMCID). No source paper on multiple sides. Exact duplicate source PNG across splits fails preflight.
- Four existing original AI source images for synthetic training, **two Gemini and two OpenAI**; a fifth OpenAI image generation is independently held out and only used in additional evaluation. These are *not* five independent model families.
- Every source panel yields a clean and an AI-spliced image in BOTH predefined conditions when forgery is valid. Primary **realistic** condition requires a visible edit region before publishing (≥0.2% edited panel area, ≥12 pixels wide and ≥4 pixels tall) and normal publication compression (native, JPEG Q82–96, or ≥80% resizing). Separate **stress** condition includes downscaling to 50–70%, JPEG Q60–78, and smaller edit regions.
- An identical, paper-disjoint *test set* is used for frozen V3 and V5, with separately calibrated 95th-percentile clean thresholds on **other** papers. AUROC and false flags are reported **overall, realistic, and stress**. No posthoc selection of only successful examples.

## Run, validation, and limitations

The Actions workflow is limited to **170 minutes** for source retrieval, benchmark construction, training and evaluation together. Preflight verifies JAX model forward/backward/serialization, masks, split independence, donor checksums and subgroup sizes before model training. Logs and resulting V5 checkpoint are archived even if later evaluation fails. The flow should not silently produce a passing result if the benchmark or model fails a check.

A successful experiment requires the **primary realistic AI-splice recall ≥50%**, realistic clean false flags **≤5%**, and V5 realistic AUROC better than frozen V3. These are exploratory go/no-go criteria, not proof of real-world reliability or statistical significance. The results must include a separately scored unchanged hard benchmark and new held-out OpenAI donor test; no production promotion is automatic.

The optional held-out donor test uses 120 pairs from one OpenAI generation excluded from training, at native, JPEG 75 and 50% resize. Correlated patches from the same generation make statistical confidence intervals optimistic. Real-world misconduct cases and generator-family-disjoint checks remain unvalidated.
